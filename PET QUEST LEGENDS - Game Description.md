PET QUEST LEGENDS - Game Description
Tagline:
Hatch legendary pets, complete epic quests, and become a legend!

Short Description (For Roblox Game Page):
Hatch legendary pets, merge them into powerful creatures, and explore magical worlds! Collect hundreds of pets, complete quests, join guilds, trade rare pets, and compete to become the ultimate legend. New worlds and pets added weekly!

Core Features:
HATCH SYSTEM

Click eggs to hatch pets
Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythical rarities
Each pet has unique designs and coin multipliers
MERGE MECHANICS

Combine 2 identical pets to create a stronger version
Merged pets have higher stats and special visual effects
Strategic depth: merge now or collect more?
PET ABILITIES

Fire pets: Melt coins faster
Flying pets: Reach high-value coin areas
Ice pets: Freeze time for bonus collection
Shadow pets: Collect coins from further away
QUEST SYSTEM

Daily quests for bonus rewards
World-specific quest chains
Achievement system with exclusive pet rewards
Legendary quests unlock mythical pets
WORLDS TO EXPLORE

Starter Forest

Space Station
Underwater Depths
Volcanic Wasteland
Crystal Caves
(More added regularly)
GUILD SYSTEM

Create or join guilds with up to 50 members
Contribute coins to unlock exclusive guild pets
Guild leaderboards and competitions
Guild-only areas with premium eggs
TRADING

Trade pets with other players
Built-in value system prevents scams
Trading hub with active marketplace
PROGRESSION

Unlock new worlds by collecting coins
Prestige system to restart with permanent bonuses
Level up your account for exclusive rewards
Climb the global leaderboards
EVENTS

Weekend events with limited-time pets
Seasonal updates (Halloween, Christmas, etc.)
Special event currencies and exclusive rewards
